Our Services

Firatech offers comprehensive STEM education programs tailored to inspire and prepare students for the technological challenges of tomorrow

Robotics Education

Building the future one robot at a time. Our robotics curriculum introduces students to the exciting world of robotics through hands-on learning experiences. From basic concepts to advanced programming and design, students develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills while building and programming their own robots.

Robotics Design

Learn mechanical design principles and build custom robots from scratch using various materials and components.

Robot Programming

Master programming languages and frameworks specifically designed for robotics applications.

Competition Prep

Prepare for national and international robotics competitions with specialized training and mentorship.

Artificial Intelligence

Harnessing the power of intelligent systems. Our AI programs demystify this cutting-edge technology through interactive learning modules. Students explore machine learning, neural networks, and AI applications while developing projects that solve real-world problems, preparing them for the future of technology.

Machine Learning

Explore supervised and unsupervised learning algorithms and implement them in real-world applications.

Neural Networks

Understand the architecture and functioning of neural networks and deep learning systems.

AI Applications

Develop AI-powered solutions for healthcare, education, business, and other sectors.

Embedded Systems

Creating intelligent hardware solutions. Students learn to design and program embedded systems using microcontrollers, sensors, and actuators. Our curriculum covers hardware-software integration, real-time operating systems, and IoT applications, providing practical skills for building smart devices.

Microcontroller Programming

Learn to program various microcontrollers using languages like C, C++, and Python for embedded applications.

IoT Development

Build Internet of Things devices and systems that communicate and interact with the cloud.

Hardware Interfacing

Master the integration of various sensors, actuators, and communication modules with microcontrollers.

Aerospace Education

Reaching for the stars through innovation. Our aerospace program introduces students to the principles of flight, aerodynamics, and space exploration. Through projects like drone building, rocket launching, and flight simulations, students gain practical understanding of aerospace engineering concepts.

Drone Technology

Design, build, and program autonomous drones for various applications including photography and mapping.

Rocketry

Learn the principles of rocket science and build your own model rockets with various propulsion systems.

Satellite Technology

Understand satellite systems and participate in CubeSat development projects.
